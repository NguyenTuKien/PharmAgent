PROMPT THIET KE TOAN BO GIAO DIEN DU AN PHARMAGENT

Ban la Senior Product Designer va Frontend Architect. Hay thiet ke day du giao dien web app PharmAgent dua tren cau truc du an hien co. Du an hien tai co frontend React + Vite con dang la template mac dinh, nhung backend/gateway/agent da co day du cac phan he nghiep vu. Hay thiet ke UI/UX theo huong san pham HealthTech that, de nguoi cao tuoi, nguoi cham soc va admin su dung hang ngay.

1. MO TA TONG THE DU AN

- Ten du an: PharmAgent.
- Muc tieu san pham:
  - Ho tro nguoi cao tuoi theo doi lich uong thuoc moi ngay.
  - Nhac nho dung gio, cho phep xac nhan da uong, bo qua, qua han hoac chua xu ly.
  - Cho phep caregiver quan ly thuoc, lich uong, theo doi tuan thu dieu tri va canh bao cho nguoi than.
  - Cung cap AI Pill Scan nhan dien thuoc theo thoi gian thuc bang camera/WebSocket.
  - Cung cap chat, goi realtime va thong bao giua caregiver va elderly.
  - Cung cap admin dashboard de quan ly user, danh muc thuoc chuan va giam sat he thong.
- Doi tuong nguoi dung:
  - Elderly: nguoi cao tuoi, can giao dien ro rang, chu lon, it thao tac, nut bam lon, muc tieu chinh la xem lich hom nay va xac nhan uong thuoc.
  - Caregiver: nguoi cham soc, can dashboard theo doi nhieu nguoi than, quan ly thuoc, lich, canh bao, thong ke va lien lac.
  - Admin: nguoi quan tri he thong, can giao dien du lieu day du, bang loc, CRUD user va CRUD danh muc thuoc.
- Kien truc he thong:
  - Frontend: React + Vite.
  - API Gateway: FastAPI, entry point cho frontend, route /api/** va /ws/**.
  - Backend core: Spring Boot, MongoDB, Redis, RabbitMQ, WebSocket/STOMP.
  - Agent service: FastAPI AI Pill Scan, WebSocket /ws/agent.
  - Cloudinary: upload avatar, anh thuoc, anh bang chung scan.
- Vai tro va phan quyen:
  - ADMIN: truy cap /api/admin/**, quan ly users va pills.
  - CAREGIVER: truy cap /api/caregiver/**, quan ly elderly, thuoc, lich, event status.
  - ELDERLY: truy cap /api/elderly/**, xac nhan uong thuoc, quan ly loi moi caregiver.
  - Moi user sau login phai chon profile dang su dung, vi access token gan voi profileId va role.

2. CAC TINH NANG HIEN CO/CAN CO

- Xac thuc va tai khoan:
  - Dang ky tai khoan voi thong tin caregiver va co the kem elderly profile.
  - Dang nhap bang email/password.
  - Chon profile sau dang nhap.
  - Refresh token, logout.
  - Quen mat khau, gui OTP email, reset password.
  - Doi mat khau.
- Ho so va thiet bi:
  - Xem danh sach profile trong tai khoan.
  - Xem/sua profile hien tai.
  - Upload/cap nhat avatar qua Cloudinary.
  - Quan ly lien he khan cap/user contacts.
  - Quan ly thiet bi dang nhap/push token.
- Ket noi Caregiver - Elderly:
  - Caregiver xem danh sach elderly da ket noi.
  - Caregiver tim elderly profile va gui loi moi ket noi.
  - Caregiver xem loi moi dang pending va cap nhat permission level.
  - Elderly xem caregiver da ket noi.
  - Elderly xem loi moi pending, chap nhan hoac tu choi.
  - Permission level gom VIEW, EDIT_SCHEDULE, MANAGE_ALL.
- Thuoc va lich uong:
  - Xem danh muc thuoc chuan.
  - Xem chi tiet thuoc.
  - Caregiver them/sua/xoa medication cho elderly.
  - Medication gom patientId, pillId, nickname, dosageAmount, dosageUnit, route, mealRelation, instruction, prescribedBy, purpose, totalQuantity, startDate, endDate, isActive.
  - Schedule gom DAILY, WEEKLY, MONTHLY, INTERVAL, CUSTOM, PRN.
  - Moi schedule co ngay bat dau/ket thuc, reminderEnabled, reminderMinutesBefore, note, daysOfWeek, frequencyInterval.
  - Moi schedule co nhieu dose time, gom takenTime va quantity.
- Dose event va tuan thu:
  - Xem dose hom nay, pending, processed.
  - Elderly xac nhan da uong thuoc.
  - Caregiver cap nhat thu cong trang thai dose.
  - Dose status gom PENDING, TAKEN, MISSED, SKIPPED, OVERDUE.
  - Thong ke adherence theo khoang ngay: total, taken, overdue, missed, skipped, pending, adherencePercent.
  - Thong ke so lieu da uong theo tung medication.
  - Canh bao ton kho thuoc sap het.
- AI Pill Scan:
  - Mo camera, stream frame base64 qua /ws/agent.
  - Nhan ket qua pillId, name, genericName, brandName, strength, confidenceScore, description, imageUrl.
  - Neu confidence >= 0.8, agent upload frame len Cloudinary va tra imageUrl.
  - Hien tai logic AI dang mock Paracetamol/Hapacol 500, nhung UI phai san sang cho model that.
- Chat, call, notification:
  - Xem chat rooms.
  - Tao/lien ket direct room voi targetProfileId.
  - Xem lich su tin nhan.
  - Gui tin nhan realtime qua STOMP /app/chat.send, subscribe /topic/room.{roomId}.
  - Nhan thong bao realtime theo /queue/user.{profileId}.notifications.
  - Goi realtime/WebRTC signaling qua /app/call.signal, subscribe /queue/user.{profileId}.call.
  - Tin nhan co type TEXT, IMAGE, SYSTEM, CALL_LOG.
- Admin:
  - Quan ly users: list, create, update, delete, lock, unlock.
  - User status gom ACTIVE, INACTIVE, LOCKED.
  - Quan ly pill catalog: create, update, delete.
  - Quan ly anh pill: them/xoa anh, viewType FRONT, BACK, PACKAGE, OTHER, primary image.
  - Giam sat he thong: gateway/backend/agent/WS health, hang doi, realtime status, loi upload/AI.

3. HUONG THEME VA DESIGN SYSTEM

- Phong cach tong the:
  - HealthTech hien dai, sach, dang tin cay, than thien voi nguoi cao tuoi.
  - Khong lam landing page marketing neu nguoi dung da dang nhap; app phai vao thang workflow chinh.
  - Giao dien can co cam giac cham soc y te, ro rang, it gay cang thang.
- Mau sac:
  - Nen chinh: off-white hoac very light blue/green (#F7FAF9, #F2F8FB).
  - Mau primary: teal/medical green (#0F8B8D hoac #0A7C72).
  - Mau secondary: calm blue (#2F80ED) cho link, chart, realtime.
  - Mau canh bao: amber (#F59E0B) cho sap den gio/sap het thuoc.
  - Mau nguy cap/loi: red/rose (#E5484D) cho missed/overdue/locked.
  - Mau thanh cong: green (#22A06B) cho taken/active/success.
  - Khong dung gradient tim xanh generic; khong de UI bi mot mau don dieu.
- Typography:
  - Font sans-serif de doc, vi du Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Manrope.
  - Elderly mode can co text lon hon: body 16-18px, nut chinh 18px, line-height rong.
  - Dashboard caregiver/admin co the dung text 14-16px de hien thi du lieu day du.
- Component rules:
  - Button chinh ro rang, co icon lucide phu hop.
  - Badge trang thai cho role, dose status, relation status, notification status, user status.
  - Card ban kinh 8px, khong long card trong card qua nhieu.
  - Bang du lieu co search, filter, pagination, sort, empty state, loading skeleton.
  - Form co label ro, validation inline, helper text, disable submit khi dang loading.
  - Modal/Drawer dung cho tao/sua nhanh, nhung cac workflow phuc tap nhu them medication nen dung wizard/routed page.
  - Realtime indicator: dot online/offline, connection status, reconnect banner.
- Accessibility:
  - Contrast cao, focus ring ro, keyboard navigable.
  - Nut thao tac quan trong co kich thuoc toi thieu 44px.
  - Elderly dashboard co che do "Large text".
  - Text va button khong duoc tran container tren mobile.
- Layout chung:
  - Desktop: app shell gom sidebar trai, topbar, content area.
  - Mobile: bottom navigation theo role, topbar gon, drawer cho menu phu.
  - Topbar: ten page, profile switcher, notification bell, chat shortcut, user avatar, logout.
  - Sidebar theo role:
    - Elderly: Hom nay, Thuoc cua toi, Scan thuoc, Caregiver, Chat, Thong bao, Ho so.
    - Caregiver: Dashboard, Nguoi than, Thuoc & lich, Lich hom nay, Thong ke, Scan thuoc, Chat, Thong bao, Ho so.
    - Admin: Overview, Users, Pill Catalog, System Health, Ho so.

- UI can co:

  - Hero co ten PharmAgent, subtitle ve quan ly lich uong thuoc va caregiver monitoring.

  - 3 khoi gia tri: Nhac lich dung gio, AI Pill Scan, Caregiver realtime.

- Chuc nang:
  - Click Dang nhap den /login.

- Layout:
  - Desktop: hero full width, text ben trai, visual/scene ben phai nhung khong lam dang card marketing qua da.
  - Mobile: logo/top actions tren cung, hero ngan gon, CTA lon.

5.2. Dang nhap

- Muc dich:
  - Xac thuc user bang email/password.
  - Sau login, hien thi buoc chon profile neu response co profiles.
- UI can co:
  - Form email, password, nut hien/an password.
  - Checkbox ghi nho dang nhap neu can.
  - Link quen mat khau.
  - Link tao tai khoan.
  - Error banner khi sai thong tin, user bi khoa hoac server loi.
  - Loading state tren nut Dang nhap.
- Chuc nang:
  - Goi POST /api/auth/login voi email/password.
  - Luu authToken va refreshToken tam thoi.
  - Dieu huong sang /select-profile neu login thanh cong.
  - Validate email format, password 6-100 ky tu.
- Layout:
  - Desktop: split layout, ben trai form, ben phai visual cham soc y te/lich thuoc.
  - Mobile: mot cot, form ro, nut lon.

5.3. Dang ky tai khoan

- Muc dich:

    - Tuy chon them elderly: firstName, lastName, phone, dateOfBirth, gender, address, avatar.
    - Cau hinh quan he: caregiverTitle, elderlyTitle, permissionLevel.
  - Upload avatar su dung presign Cloudinary.
  - Validation inline theo rule backend.
  - Summary truoc khi submit.
- Chuc nang:
  - Goi POST /api/auth/signup.
  - Neu dang ky thanh cong, dieu huong ve /login hoac login flow.
  - Cho phep bo qua buoc elderly neu user chi muon tao caregiver truoc.
- Layout:
  - Desktop: stepper doc hoac ngang, form section ro rang.
  - Mobile: tung buoc rieng, nut Tiep tuc/Quay lai fixed bottom.

5.4. Quen mat khau

- Muc dich:

- UI can co:
  - Form nhap email.

- Chuc nang:

  - Mot card form nho, tap trung, text huong dan ngan gon.
